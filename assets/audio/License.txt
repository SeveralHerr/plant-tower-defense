	ONE THING THAT LOOKS LIKE A PACK AND IS NOT, recorded here so the question is
	not re-opened by the next person to run `ogginfo` over this directory. The two
	.ogg cues carry Vorbis comments that read like a numbered pack:

	  plant-place.ogg      TITLE=place10   MP3GAIN_UNDO=+002,+002,N
	  clickanddrag.ogg     TITLE=place6    MP3GAIN_UNDO=-005,-005,N

	A numbered TITLE out of a set, a batch gain-normalisation tag, and NO
	ARTIST -- where every Kenney file in this directory carries ARTIST=Kenney and
	COMMENT=www.kenney.nl. That combination is the usual fingerprint of a pack
	file, and it is what prompted the bead. It is an artefact of the owner's own
	take-numbering and mastering pass, not of a pack. The four .wav cues carry no
	metadata at all (bare 44.1kHz stereo PCM), so there is nothing to mistake --
	chomper-bite.wav was checked the same way when it arrived: RIFF chunks `fmt `
	and `data` and nothing else, 0.337s of 16-bit stereo at 44.1kHz.
